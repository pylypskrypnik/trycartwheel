FAQ

Frequently
asked questions

What does Cartwheel do?

Cartwheel is a delivery orchestration and management platform. Restaurant chains and other delivery-heavy businesses use it to manage in-house drivers, third-party delivery providers, or both from a single system. Cartwheel decides who delivers each order automatically, based on rules the operator sets.

Does Cartwheel provide delivery drivers?

No. Cartwheel does not hire or employ drivers and does not deliver orders itself. It is the software layer that manages your own drivers and connects you to third-party fleets like Uber Direct, DoorDash Drive, Grubhub, Skipcart, and 50+ local providers.

What is Hybrid Delivery?

Hybrid Delivery means using in-house drivers and third-party providers at the same time, with rules deciding which orders go where. For example: in-house drivers handle orders within 3 miles, and everything else routes to a third-party fleet. Cartwheel applies your rules to every order automatically.

Which POS and ordering systems does Cartwheel integrate with?

Cartwheel integrates with Toast, PAR, Olo, Qu, ChowNow, MonkeyMedia, and Onosys, plus catering platforms including ezCater, Olo Catering, and Toast Catering & Events. Orders flow from your existing stack into Cartwheel with no change to how customers order.

Does Cartwheel work for catering orders?

Yes. Cartwheel is built for catering delivery: pre-scheduled orders, pre-delivery checklists, photo and signature capture, a 20-mile delivery radius, and access to DoorDash Large Order Fulfillment plus dozens of catering-specific providers, with a 97% average on-time rate. It also meets ezCater's tracking requirements.

Is Cartwheel enterprise-ready?

Yes. Cartwheel is SOC 2 certified, maintains 99.9% platform uptime, and powers delivery for enterprise brands including Portillo's, P.F. Chang's, Potbelly, and 25+ of NRN's Top 500 restaurants.

How much does Cartwheel cost?

Cartwheel pricing starts at $65 per location per month in the US, with volume-based rates and negotiated enterprise pricing. Pre-negotiated third-party delivery rates, up to 27% lower than going direct, often offset the software cost.
