FAQ

Frequently
asked questions

What is third-party delivery management?

It is the software layer between your ordering channels and outside delivery fleets. Cartwheel receives the order from your POS or ordering platform, picks the provider that can deliver it best on price and pickup ETA, tracks the driver to the door, and files refunds when a delivery goes wrong.

Which third-party delivery providers does Cartwheel work with?

Uber Direct, DoorDash Drive, Grubhub, Skipcart, and 50+ local and regional providers, including catering-capable fleets and DoorDash Large Order Fulfillment. One integration connects all of them.

Do I need my own drivers to use Cartwheel?

No. You can run fully third-party from day one. If you add in-house drivers later, the same rules engine starts splitting orders between your fleet and third-party providers without changing systems.

How does Cartwheel decide which provider gets an order?

For every order Cartwheel compares live price and pickup ETA across every connected provider and commits to the best one in about 0.4 seconds. You set whether best means cheapest, fastest, or a balance, and you can pin preferred providers by location.

Are third-party rates through Cartwheel lower than going direct?

Up to 27% lower. Rates are pre-negotiated across the network through aggregated client volume, so a single location gets pricing normally reserved for large chains.

What happens when a third-party delivery is late or never arrives?

Cartwheel files the refund claim automatically against a refund matrix you set, and reports the affected orders and customers. Brands recover an average of $0.29 per third-party order without anyone chasing a support queue.

Can customers still track the order on my brand?

Yes. Every delivery, including third-party ones, ends on a tracking page carrying your logo, your colors, and your promotions, followed by a review prompt that reaches your team in real time.

Can large catering orders go to third-party fleets?

Yes. Set a dispatch rule by order value or item count and large orders route to catering-capable fleets, including DoorDash Large Order Fulfillment and 50+ catering providers, so a 50-sandwich order gets a vehicle that fits it.
