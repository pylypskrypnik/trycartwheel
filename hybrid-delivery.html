Cartwheel's Hybrid Delivery rules engine supports over one billion possible rule combinations, including:

  • Order throttling: Cap orders per driver. 2 orders × 4 drivers = 8; the ninth routes to a third-party provider.
  • Order size: Deliver in-house only above a minimum, for example orders of $250 or more.
  • Compare pickup ETA: Dispatch whoever arrives fastest, in-house or third-party.
  • Delivery radius: Beyond your limit, for example 3 miles, orders route to a third-party provider.
  • Driver availability: If every in-house driver is busy or off, orders route out automatically.
  • Time of day: In-house for lunch and dinner only, or weekends only. Your call.
  • Dynamic zones: Zones that flex by order value: under $20 within 1 mile; $100 or more up to 5 miles.
  • Stack the rules: Every rule works with the others, so one policy covers every shift and location.

Set rules globally, by region, or per location. A downtown store and a suburban store can run different policies under one brand standard.

FAQ

Frequently
asked questions

What is Hybrid Delivery?

Hybrid Delivery means running in-house drivers and third-party providers at the same time, with rules deciding which orders go where. You set the conditions once: order value, distance, driver availability, time of day, throttle limits. Cartwheel routes every incoming order to whichever fleet fits it.

How does Cartwheel decide which fleet gets an order?

The rules engine evaluates your conditions on every incoming order and commits to a fleet in under one second per decision. Rules stack, so one policy can cover order value, distance, availability, and shift at the same time.

Do I need my own drivers to run Hybrid Delivery?

No. Many brands start fully third-party and add in-house drivers later. The same engine handles both, so adding a fleet is a rules change, not a systems change.

Can rules be different for each location?

Yes. Rules can be set globally, by region, or per location, so a downtown store and a suburban one can run different policies under the same brand standard.

What happens when all of my drivers are busy?

Driver availability is a rule. When every in-house driver is busy or off shift, orders route to a third-party provider automatically instead of queuing or forcing you to pause delivery.

Are third-party rates through Cartwheel lower than going direct?

Up to 27% lower. Rates are pre-negotiated across the network on aggregated client volume, and you pay no marketplace commission on in-house or third-party orders.

Does the customer see who delivered the order?

They see you. Every delivery, in-house or third-party, tracks on a page carrying your logo, your colors, and your promotions, followed by a review prompt that reaches your team in real time.

What do I need to get started?

Your existing stack. Cartwheel connects to Toast, PAR, Olo, Qu, ChowNow, MonkeyMedia, and Onosys, plus catering platforms including ezCater, Olo Catering, and Toast Catering & Events. Thousands of drivers can be onboarded in days.
